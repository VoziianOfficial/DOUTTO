Privacy Policy for DOUTTO

DOUTTO is an independent appliance repair provider matching platform. It is not a direct appliance repair company, contractor, or local repair team. This page describes how information may be handled when visitors browse the website, submit a request, or contact the platform.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through this website. By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how the platform may handle information.

The purpose of the website is to help homeowners compare local appliance repair provider options. DOUTTO does not perform repair work directly and does not employ the providers that may be listed, referenced, or connected through the platform.

2. Information We May Collect

When you use this website or submit a request form, the platform may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • ZIP code or general service area
  • Appliance type
  • Short description of the appliance issue
  • Messages or questions submitted through the website

The website may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URLs, approximate location derived from technical signals, cookie preferences, and general usage activity.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate the website, respond to requests, improve visitor experience, and support the appliance repair provider comparison process.

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to contact form submissions
  • Helping process requests for provider comparison options
  • Understanding appliance category and location context
  • Improving website layout, usability, and content
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse
  • Complying with applicable legal or operational requirements

4. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers, platform vendors, operational partners, or independent local providers when reasonably necessary to process a request or support the provider comparison experience.

Any independent provider is separate from DOUTTO. Homeowners are responsible for verifying provider licensing, insurance, pricing, quote terms, warranties, availability, and credentials before choosing any company.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, to protect legal rights, to prevent misuse, or to respond to lawful requests.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies, localStorage, and similar technologies to remember cookie preferences, improve user experience, understand visitor interactions, and maintain basic site functionality.

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies through the cookie banner where available. Browser settings may also allow you to block or delete cookies.

For more information, please review the Cookie Policy.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through the website. However, some request or contact features may not function without the information needed to process your inquiry.

You may also contact the platform to ask questions about information you submitted through the website.

Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not needed for an appliance repair provider comparison request.
