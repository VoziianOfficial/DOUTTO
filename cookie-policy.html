2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember preferences, support functionality, understand visitor behavior, and improve user experience.

Similar technologies may include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, or scripts that help the website function or measure interactions.

3. How We May Use Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Remembering cookie consent choices
  • Supporting basic website functionality
  • Improving website performance and usability
  • Understanding which pages visitors interact with
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse
  • Improving content, layout, and user experience

Cookie use does not mean that DOUTTO performs appliance repairs directly or guarantees any provider work.
